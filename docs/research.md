# Research notes

Short context for why Northway is a consultancy template, and what the earlier research does and does not prove. Full argument: [docs/proposal.md](proposal.md). Round-1 Page: [Skala templates, market research](https://cursor.com/canvas/store-f58eb506-6e10-48fb-a7af-e0adb21f64a9/f6dc1261-5f6e-4d9f-a922-dba553d6a5fd). Compiled 12 November 2026, Europe/Stockholm.

## Why this template

Skala's first sellable template is a calm, premium website kit for people who need a studio-quality site and cannot design one themselves. The buyer is a consultant, a strategist, or a small non-design agency (marketing, development, architecture or interior, copywriting), usually a team of one to ten.

That is a correction of the first pass. The first pass looked at what sells on the Framer Marketplace and which businesses are likely to launch and grow, and it picked "boutique studios, agencies and consultants" as the first product, with AI SaaS as the next. Designers do buy templates, but they are the weaker buyer for this kit, because many of them want to build their own site. The stronger buyer cannot design and still wants a site that looks like a trusted consultancy: work with a result, a clear offer, a published price, and a short enquiry form.

The demo studio, also named Northway, is a small strategy and growth consultancy covering positioning, brand strategy and marketing. Buyers swap the name and the content after purchase.

## What carried over

- A work-first, calm, typographic site is still the right feel. That came from live studio and consultancy sites (Pentagram, AREA 17, Gretel, Clay, Andersen, Halston and others) and is recorded in [design/REFERENCE-BOARD.md](../design/REFERENCE-BOARD.md).
- Professional buyers will pay for a finished, premium kit if it saves them a custom build. Editions at $149 (Code), $199 (Code plus Figma) and $249 (Code plus Figma plus Framer) are the product shape Amos has confirmed.
- Form-only enquiry, light theme only, and a clear process with a published price all came from conversion research and from this buyer's needs, not from a visual-portfolio habit.
- Writing (Insights) is a stronger proof path for strategists and consultants than a photograph-led portfolio. That is why Insights is a primary nav item and a Home section.

## What does not carry over, unaltered

The marketplace share, list prices and "paid hold" figures in the proposal were observed in the **design-studio and agency-portfolio** category. They are the closest comps for a studio-style template, not demand proof for non-design consultants.

"They buy Figma" was a fit argument for design studios and should not be assumed here. Framer, a CMS-like content model, and an implementation offer are the stronger product fit for this buyer. The Figma edition is unproven for them. That is one reason week one ships the **Code edition only**, with Figma and Framer later.

skala.design is weaker proof for this product, because Skala is a design studio and designers are not the main buyer. Craft and IA can still be shown.

The specialist professional-services column is closer to this audience than the original "design studio" pick, but the research did not isolate consultants who cannot design. It found that category more price-sensitive: Webflow Professional Services templates had a median around **$79**. Willingness-to-pay estimates in the round-1 research were not gathered from this buyer.

**Do not treat the $149 to $249 bundle as evidenced for this audience.** Amos has confirmed those prices and wants a premium template to launch in a week. Keep the prices. Keep the caveat honest. Do not invent new market figures to justify them.
