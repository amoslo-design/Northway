# Northway v2: decisions (approved by Amos, 2026-11-13)

Codex's first build was rejected as generic. These decisions replace the v1 brand where they conflict.

1. **Direction:** Eden-inspired. Pine green `#1F3A2E` bands (with fine grain) on a **white `#FFFFFF` canvas**, with light grey **`#E8E8E4` as the elevated surface** (cards, panels, inputs; no drop shadows). Updated 2026-11-13, replaces the earlier `#F2F2EF` base. Light theme stays the base; green is a section, not a dark site.
2. **Display font:** Bricolage Grotesque (500/600). Body and UI: Hanken Grotesk. No serif. Mona Sans is NOT used.
3. **Demo labelling:** remove every per-item "Sample" badge, "for demo only" prefix and builder caption. One site-level disclosure in the footer: "Northway is a demonstration studio that ships with this template." Empty blocks are hidden, never shown as empty cards.
4. Copper, oxblood, warm paper and Newsreader/Fraunces from the earlier drafts are dropped.
5. Motion language (`design/MOTION.md`) is an outline; implement only what it marks as allowed, and respect reduced motion.

Where any of the files in this folder still say "Bricolage (or Mona Sans)" or "pending confirmation", read it as decided above.


## v3 decisions (2026-11-13, approved by Amos after reviewing Codex's v2 build)

Codex's v2 build still read as AI-generated. These override anything earlier:

1. **Colour:** white `#FFFFFF` canvas, elevated grey `#E8E8E4`, no drop shadows. Pine bands unchanged.
2. **No numbering anywhere:** no process steps 1-4, no case index 01-04, no step or index numerals of any kind.
3. **No monogram:** the giant 'K' card is removed. The hero gets a grey Excerpt panel with a named client quote. Each quote appears once per page.
4. **Rhythm:** no two consecutive sections share a section head or a layout; at least two Home sections are not grids. The heading-left/link-right head appears at most once. Uppercase eyebrow only in the hero.
5. **Case Ledger:** one editorial featured case plus quieter entries, not identical hairline rows. Do not repeat the hero result.
6. **Process Path:** four named stages, uneven widths, no numerals.
7. **CTAs:** at most three primary 'Start a project' CTAs on Home (hero, Grow, Pine Band).
8. **Pine Band heading:** keep **"Ready when you are."** (Amos's choice). Drop the floating cream waymark above it.
