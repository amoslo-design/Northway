# Northway v3: Refero references

Researched 13 November 2026, Europe/Stockholm, from the Refero styles library (`refero_search_styles`, `refero_get_style`) and from preview keyframes of those styles. Refero's screen search did not return these studio or advisory sites (it surfaced unrelated SaaS product UIs), so section patterns below come from each style's observed recipes and from the local frames in `refs/`. Every citation is something Refero actually returned. Nothing here is invented.

**How to use this.** Northway's tokens are already locked (white canvas, elevated grey, pine bands, Bricolage + Hanken, no serif, no numbering, no monogram). Do not copy anyone's palette, typefaces, wordmark, photography or copy. Take craft: spacing, type scale, layout moves, surface rules. Synthesize; one primary direction, two narrow borrows. If a reference conflicts with v3 decisions, v3 wins.

Primary direction: **Displace**, for a white reading canvas and one concentrated deep-green invitation. Borrow ledger discipline and reading-size proof from **Munro Partners**. Borrow unboxed entries and generous intervals from **V-A-C**. Borrow sparse text and a quiet utility ending from **Walden**.

## A. Style references

### 1. Displace: "gallery meets operating manual" (primary)

- **Site:** [Displace Agency](https://displace.agency) (displace.agency)
- **Style UUID:** `fe0ac316-3a0e-4041-9da6-39c05efe2d59`
- **Why it is close:** White paper and black ink as the structural majority. Deep green (`#048060` in the source) is reserved for a single full-width invitation band, not sprinkled through every section. Offer cards are flat pale grey (`#ededed`) with **no drop shadow**. Actions are short rectangles (36px high, 3px corners), not pills. Regular-weight grotesk does the display work (64px/62px, tracking -0.045em).
- **Take:** Colour concentration (one green band, then stop). Elevation by fill, not shadow. Regular-weight display, tight tracking. Almost full-width composition (28.8px gutters), not every chapter boxed to 1200px. A substantial closing, not a legal strip. Proof as a short contextual line, not a dashboard of giant metrics.
- **Do not copy:** The floating glass search bar and 261px contact launcher. The 3-column rainbow capability mosaic (blue, red, violet, leaf green). Pill-free is good; their 3px corners and black closing canvas are theirs. Suisse Intl, their wordmark, Shopify-product photography, and any Displace copy. The "Talk to us" persistent chip.

### 2. Munro Partners: "landscape meets ledger"

- **Site:** [Munro Partners](https://munropartners.com) (munropartners.com)
- **Style UUID:** `83fcbacf-7eb7-49ce-b7f8-07f5f6f4d3e3`
- **Why it is close:** An advisory voice with the discipline of a financial ledger. Fine 1px rules, regular-weight neo-grotesk (68px opening, 43px statements, 16px/24px body), evidence that stays at reading size. Wide chapters alternate a full-width ledger with half-width substance beside open ground. Suited to considered, information-rich services.
- **Take:** Facts at body size, aligned in ruled rows. Open field beside substance (do not fill every gap). Small rectangular outline actions (2px corners, 8px 12px padding), arrow as part of the label. "We" voice, short sentence-case headlines, no superlatives. A continuous deep-colour legal band after a utility close.
- **Do not copy:** The oat/cream canvas (`#fff9ee`) or the teal, plum, lemon, lavender shelf. Northway is white, with grey as the elevated surface. The rotated word sculpture is their identity; do not invent a vertical "Northway" rail. Category badges ("Managed fund", "ETF"). Giant returns or a colourful resource shelf on Home. Numbered principle markers (see Process). Their landscape photography.

### 3. Walden: "objects in stillness"

- **Site:** [Walden](https://walden.us) (walden.us)
- **Style UUID:** `c6f2c718-4cef-4e20-bc4b-448ea995a50c`
- **Why it is close:** White canvas, charcoal ink, regular-weight grotesk (Graphik 54px/1.06). Sparse text. Editorial proof is unboxed: 1px warm-grey top rules, 22px padding, 56px column gaps, **no drop shadows** under the columns. Closing thought gets 136px of vertical breathing room.
- **Take:** A neutral frame; personality lives in content, not in UI chrome. Fine rules instead of filled feature cards. 68px editorial section padding, 40px gutters. Solid actions at 52px with 2px corners. An isolated closing sentence before any form. Captions stay small (13.5px) so images feel large.
- **Do not copy:** A photography-first commerce site. Northway's Home hero is a pine band with a named result, not a full-bleed product photograph. Do not pull forest greens or cobalt out of the pictures and into the interface (the style itself forbids this). Equal three-column product tiles. Numbered circular markers on process columns. Pill-shaped "Live chat" controls. A requirement for lifestyle photography when a case has none (use the Excerpt instead).

### 4. V-A-C Foundation: "a wired cultural index"

- **Site:** [V-A-C Foundation](https://v-a-c.org) (v-a-c.org)
- **Style UUID:** `f8a0de9f-438a-442f-b7d4-5e48e977be96`
- **Why it is close:** Austere white editorial system. Information is dense; **150px of white** sits between rows instead of hairlines or cards. Titles wrap to their real length. Colour stays inside pictures. A shallow `#f1f1f1` utility band closes the page, not a giant conversion banner. No soft shadows, no rounded media, no raised panels.
- **Take:** Space as the separator. Uneven title lengths. "See more" as a bare text action at the **end** of the list, not opposite the heading. A quiet grey utility layer. Text-first archive: titles, not thumbnails, can carry a body of work.
- **Do not copy:** The connected letter-and-rule masthead (V joined to A to C by a 2px line) or the rotated navigation rail. That is their identity. A three-column picture grid as the only Home work treatment. Opening with no hero claim and no primary action (Northway's Briefing still leads with a named result, from-price and one CTA). Diagrammatic dashed wiring as decoration. Their custom Diagramatika type.

## B. Hero patterns

Northway's Briefing is already decided: a centred pine band with grain, a named result at display size, one white pill, a from-price, and a grey Excerpt overlapping the white page. Use these references for craft around that decision, not to replace it.

### Munro Partners, landscape opening

- **Image:** [`refs/munro-hero.jpg`](refs/munro-hero.jpg)
- **Observed:** A 620px edge-to-edge landscape. Regular 68px/1 headline, "Invest in the journey," near the lower left, about one third of the width. No conventional top nav bar on the image; a 52px Menu rail sits below. **No hero button cluster.** Within the first 900px: the image, the rail, and a 30px introductory statement. Proof begins below the fold.
- **Take:** One concise promise. Regular-weight display. No second pill, no outline chips, no slogan stack. Proof is allowed to wait until the next chapter.
- **Do not copy:** Scenery as the hero (ours is pine). The rotated word sculpture. Zero CTAs (we still have one: "Start a project"). A top-heavy nav over the band.

### Walden, room-scale promise

- **Image:** [`refs/walden-hero.jpg`](refs/walden-hero.jpg)
- **Observed:** A 774px full-width photograph. White copy in the lower-left half: 54px/1.06 regular title in a 620px block, a narrower lead (~439px) 20px below, one outline action 30px after that. A thin proof divider near the bottom. The next editorial section stays below the fold.
- **Take:** One immersive idea, one short promise, **one** action. Reserved space for the copy. Support text at a narrower measure than the title.
- **Do not copy:** Replacing the pine Briefing with a photo hero. Several competing actions. A huge centred headline, a split-screen text panel, or a floating dashboard collage (the style lists these as things to avoid).

### Displace, cinematic / case opening (style recipe)

- **Style recipe:** "Cinematic promise opening" and the case-study variant. 810px media stage, left-aligned 64px/62px regular headline, 16px/21px support, a compact proof strip and 36px actions before the stage ends. Case studies set a project title over photography. Avoid a "centred SaaS headline above a laptop."
- **Take:** Regular-weight display at real size. Proof as a short sentence (Northway already withdrew the outline chips). Actions visible in the first viewport, and only one primary.
- **Do not copy:** A name-as-billboard opening ("Lucas Zito" at huge size). Their glass toolbar. Full-bleed black as the Home hero.

## C. Case study and work lists (not card grids)

v3 already specifies the Case Ledger: one editorial featured case (Excerpt or photograph) plus quieter text entries, uneven measure, space instead of hairlines, "See all case studies" at the **end**. No indexes, no row eyebrows.

Honest note: several Refero work lists are still equal columns or equal rows. The useful craft is elsewhere: unboxed entries, space, uneven writing, a featured/quiet contrast, and "see all" at the end. Do not import a three-column project grid or a uniform fund table onto Home.

### Munro Partners, orientation + ruled facts

- **Image:** [`refs/munro-orientation.jpg`](refs/munro-orientation.jpg)
- **Observed:** A full-width 30px introductory statement, then a split: media on the left, a 43px line ("Our goal is to be your global growth manager.") and body paragraphs on the right, then **ruled rows** whose values stay at 16px (Funds under management, returns, team). A dated footnote under the last rule. The left label is a small uppercase "Who we are."
- **Take:** The split (substance beside open or media). Facts at body size, not display. A footnote for conditions. This craft also helps the Price Tablet: comparable figures in a sheet, not three plan-cards.
- **Do not copy:** Uppercase section labels as a site-wide system (Northway allows a tracked eyebrow only in the hero). Investment figures or "Who we are" as a heading pattern repeated on every chapter. The YouTube thumbnail chrome.

### Munro Partners, full-width ledger

- **Image:** [`refs/munro-ledger.jpg`](refs/munro-ledger.jpg)
- **Observed:** Full-width comparison rows: name, one-line caption, a small colour badge, three 16px figures, a 1px rule under each row. "See more" sits at the lower right, after the list. Numbers never become display type.
- **Take:** Reading-size evidence. A text link at the **end** of the list. Captions in sentence case under the name. For quiet Case Ledger entries: client, one-line result, a muted caption ("Health, 2025"), then space.
- **Do not copy:** This exact rhythm as the Home work section. It is four-plus identical hairline rows, which is the tell v3 rejected. No category badges. No two-digit indexes either (this frame luckily has none; do not add them). Do not make every case a comparable percentage.

### V-A-C, open editorial rows

- **Image:** [`refs/vac-editorial-rows.jpg`](refs/vac-editorial-rows.jpg)
- **Observed:** White field. Picture entries in three columns, 20px gutters. Metadata above the image, 34px/0.9 regular titles below. Titles wrap to different heights. **150px** of white between rows. No section background, no card chrome, no shadows. There is no hero slogan and no primary button on this opening (see Don't).
- **Take:** Intervals instead of rules. Titles that are allowed to run long. For a **Case studies index** page, a comparable, unboxed catalogue can work. For Home, use this as the quietness of the secondary entries, not as a three-up grid of featured work.
- **Do not copy:** Three equal picture columns as the Home Ledger. Opening Home with no result and no CTA. The wired masthead. Truncating titles to force identical caption heights (the style forbids this).

### V-A-C, alternating connected archive (style recipe, no keyframe in this set)

- **Style recipe:** "Alternating connected archive" and "Dashed chronology." A vertical spine through a 940px field. Entries alternate left and right. **Titles, not thumbnails, carry the content.** The spine stops at the last entry, then white space, then the utility footer.
- **Take:** A text-first body of work. A single spine that ends with the content (useful also for Process). Uneven placement, not a grid. For quiet Home entries, writing can stand without a panel.
- **Do not copy:** The dashed museum-diagram look as decoration. Dates as a second numbering system. A timeline graphic behind every case.

### Displace, case-study gallery (style recipe)

- **Style recipe:** "Case-study gallery chapter." Image chapters **alternate** full plates, horizontal miniatures and paired portraits, with 30px padding and 120px pauses under captions. "Choose the arrangement that suits the material rather than repeating one card layout."
- **Take:** The featured case may be editorial and large; the next cases must not repeat its layout. Variation is the point.
- **Do not copy:** The "Related projects" three-column portrait row on the same site (equal cards, small captions). That is fine as a case-page exit; it is not the Home Ledger.

## D. Process and how-we-work (without numerals)

v3: four named stages (Intro, Plan, Strategy, Handover), uneven widths, no numerals, one spine, Strategy in a grey elevated panel.

Honest note: the Refero process recipes we opened still use equal columns, numbered markers, or both. Take the unboxed, named, compact writing. Reject the numbers and the even widths.

### Munro Partners, statement and principles (style recipe)

- **Style recipe:** "Statement and principles," used on method and philosophy pages. A 43px statement spans the page, then four 335px columns with 20px gaps and **fine right borders**. Each item has a 22px title and 16px body. The source allows "a small line symbol **or a 30px numbered marker**."
- **Take:** A short page-width statement, then named stages with compact descriptions. Fine dividers, not boxes. Keep each description short enough to scan.
- **Do not copy:** The 30px numbered marker, the word "Step," or four equal 335px columns. Strategy must be visually larger. Do not use colourful icon discs (the style also forbids this).

### Walden, three-step reassurance (style recipe)

- **Style recipe:** A heading, then three ruled process columns. Subheading and body explain each step. The **only** structure is a 1px top rule (no shadows, no filled cards). A ruled action strip may follow after 76px.
- **Take:** Unboxed columns organised by a top rule. Names as subheadings. One optional text link after the stages, not a primary CTA in this section (Home already spends its primaries in the hero, Grow, and the Pine Band).
- **Do not copy:** "Small dark circular numbered markers sitting on their top lines." The style's own transfer even says to use circular markers for process; we do not. Three equal columns. Feature-icon badges (also forbidden by the style).

### V-A-C, dashed spine (style recipe, applied narrowly)

- **Style recipe:** A vertical spine that **terminates at the final entry**, with clear white before the footer. Horizontal leaders are theirs.
- **Take:** One continuous spine behind the four names, ending with Handover. No waymark on every step.
- **Do not copy:** Dashed museum leaders, alternating left/right dates, or a decorative diagram that does not explain the work.

## E. Testimonial and quote treatments

Each quote appears **once** per page. The hero Excerpt is a grey `#E8E8E4` panel with a named client quote, optional short pine tick (not a full-height bar), no monogram.

### Displace, editorial quote

- **Image:** [`refs/displace-quote.jpg`](refs/displace-quote.jpg)
- **Observed (this frame):** A black case-study stage. A short vertical hairline on the left. A display-size quote (32px in the style recipe): "Design objects need a gallery before they need a cart. We built the gallery first and let Shopify wait quietly behind it." Attribution under it, two quiet lines: "Displace" and "Studio note." Large empty field around the block. This matches the style recipe "Editorial note and specifications": a 960px centred note, 1px graphite left rule, 80px left padding, 32px quote, small two-line attribution, 120px vertical padding. "A quote is not a testimonial unless attributed accordingly."
- **Take:** Left tick, not a full-height bar. Quote at heading or lead size, readable measure. Named attribution in smaller muted text. Generous padding. Use this structure for the hero Excerpt (on grey, ink type) and for a featured no-image case. Do not restate the hero result in the same words.
- **Do not copy:** The black full-bleed as the Home Excerpt ground (ours is elevated grey on white, peeking from pine). The **Services** and **Features** chip rows under the quote in this frame: those are outline tags, which v3 withdrew. "Studio note" as a substitute for a named client. A second appearance of the same quote lower on the page.

### Munro Partners, writing cadence (style recipe)

- **Style writing rules:** Short sentence-case headlines; confidence from named methods and qualified facts, not superlatives. Avoid: revolutionary, game-changing, guaranteed, crush, unlock unlimited, hurry. Separate the promise from the proof; do not make every paragraph sound like a headline.
- **Take:** The voice discipline. A quote should be a specific sentence a real person could have said, attributed with a name and a role or studio, once.
- **Do not copy:** Munro's own headlines or claims.

## F. CTA band and footer

v3: at most three primary "Start a project" CTAs on Home (hero, Grow, Pine Band). Pine Band heading stays **"Ready when you are."** Continuous with the footer. One site-level disclosure in the footer. No floating waymark above the heading.

### Displace, green invitation (style recipe) + substantial closing

- **Image (closing):** [`refs/displace-closing.jpg`](refs/displace-closing.jpg)
- **Green invitation (recipe, not in this keyframe set):** An offer-split band, about 382px tall, 90px vertical padding. 48px statement on the left, 24px support on the right, then a primary button. Deep green is this interval only. "Use the coloured interval for a low-friction next step with explicit practical conditions, not a generic slogan."
- **Closing (this frame, and the recipe):** A spacious dark ending. "Want to talk about your project?" with an email field. A real reply-time sentence: "Share your email. We reply within 2 working hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 Madrid. This is not a newsletter signup." A narrow link column on the left; the right half is deliberately empty. Locale, social, email and legal along the baseline.
- **Take:** Pine Band as that concentrated invitation: one statement (the approved heading), one white pill, practical reassurance, then a quieter footer on the same green. 105px-class top padding and a wide measure for the heading (source: 720px). A genuine reply-time line in the Enquiry, not in a second banner. Breathing room on the trailing side.
- **Do not copy:** Their black ending (ours is pine, continuous). The "Tell us about the project" floating field at the top of this frame. "The Displace Ecosystem" product panels. A 480px arrow button. Translucent reassurance chips on the green band. Filling the empty right half.

### Munro Partners, utility close + deep legal band

- **Images:** [`refs/munro-closing.jpg`](refs/munro-closing.jpg), [`refs/munro-legal-band.jpg`](refs/munro-legal-band.jpg)
- **Observed:** Left: a 43px "Subscribe to our newsletter" and a form. Right: four sitemap columns. About 745px of utility, then a 353px deep teal band with policy links, a small mark, and dense disclosure. The colourful four-card shelf sits **above** this ending.
- **Take:** Asymmetry (form or invitation on one side, utility links on the other). A deep-colour band that is continuous with the close, carrying disclosure. This is the closest structural cousin of Pine Band plus footer disclosure.
- **Do not copy:** The plum/lilac/ochre/lemon cards. A newsletter-heavy close as the main ask (our primary is "Start a project"). A wall of legal prose as a visual feature; one honest disclosure sentence is enough. Teal.

### Walden, open-line ending (contrast)

- **Style recipe, and the quieter craft in Walden's closing frames:** An isolated 31px/1.45 statement in a 780px measure with 136px padding, then a narrow form, then a 287px white footer: a full-width underlined email field, four link groups, a small copyright, unused space. "End without a second promotional banner." "Do not make the footer a dark, oversized sales panel."
- **Take:** Breathing room around the closing thought. Utility links that look like links. No second primary in the footer itself (the Pine Band above it is the last primary).
- **Do not copy:** Dropping the Pine Band in favour of a white-only close. Walden's "do not make a dark sales footer" is about not stuffing a leftover CTA slab into the legal area; it does not override the approved pine invitation.

### V-A-C, continuation + grey utility footer

- **Image:** [`refs/vac-footer.jpg`](refs/vac-footer.jpg)
- **Observed:** After the last editorial row, a centred bare-text continuation ("Load more" in the source language), a large white interval, then a shallow grey band (~212px). Four groups: address and contact, organisational links, a spare email field, policy links. No campaign slogan in the footer.
- **Take:** The next-step link at the end of the work, then space, then utility. A footer that does not shout.
- **Do not copy:** Grey as the Pine Band (ours is pine). Omitting the invitation chapter. Their masthead line running through the footer.
