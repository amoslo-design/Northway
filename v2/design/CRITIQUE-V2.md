# Northway — Critique of Codex v2

**Proposal only.** Written 13 November 2026, Europe/Stockholm, from `/workspace/northway-v2/codex-v2-screenshot.png` (1440×7039). Does not change the repo or Figma. This file is the visual and brand critique. Layout, rhythm and structural alternatives live in [`/workspace/northway-v2/docs/UX-CRITIQUE-V2.md`](/workspace/northway-v2/docs/UX-CRITIQUE-V2.md) (UX Lead, in progress). The v3 visual direction for the key sections is already in [`NOTES-FOR-UX.md`](NOTES-FOR-UX.md).

Amos’s verdict: it looks “super AI generated with the numbering, the layout etc.” He wants a **white canvas**, with **grey as the elevated colour**. That is correct, and it is not the only problem. The page is a competent assembly of our own components, stamped the same way six times. It does not look like a studio that made a decision.

Be blunt: several of the strongest tells were **invited by our v2 docs**. Those invitations are withdrawn in this pass. PRINCIPLES, BRAND-V2 and MOTION are revised to match. UX Lead is also tightening CONVERSION.md rules (fixed section anatomy, numbered steps, a CTA in every section) that pushed the same way.

The rebuild this file describes is **v3**. Colour, type, imagery, texture and the visual treatment of each component are specified here and in BRAND-V2. For a pure layout or structure tell, the fix is short and points to UX Lead.

---

## What this build got right

Credit where it is due, so the rebuild does not throw the good work out.

- The Briefing is a centred pine band with grain (sampled; it is there), cream Bricolage at real display size, the Kindred result first, a from-price in the cluster, and **one** white pill. That is the right hero idea.
- The Price Tablet is **one sheet**, not three SaaS plan-cards. Grow is marked with a pine rule and “TYPICAL.” Position and Brand + growth are text links; only Grow has the pill.
- Case studies are text-first, fully written, with no Sample badges. The footer disclosure is in the right place, on continuous pine, with the old slogan retired from the display line.
- Body copy and the Kindred, Holloway, Eastport and Cedar lines are specific. Type pairing (Bricolage display, Hanken body) is in place.
- Phosphor is quiet. There is no icon-beside-every-heading, no mesh, no orbiting chips, no serif costume, no black CTA slab on a black footer.

The ingredients were mostly right. The page still reads as a generated template because of how they are **repeated, numbered, and chrome-ed**.

## Ranked tells

Ranked by how much each one makes the page look generated. Every tell Amos or Bodhi named is here, plus every other one visible in the screenshot. Each has a specific visual fix Codex can implement.

### 1. Process Path numbered 1 2 3 4, in four equal columns

**The tell Amos named first.** A large Bricolage numeral, a waymark, a connecting hairline, a short name and a short paragraph, four times, perfectly even. It is the “How we work” module from every AI landing kit. Below it, a second white card restates Strategy (“Strategy your team can put to work.”). The section says Strategy twice and still looks like a template.

**We invited this.** BRAND-V2 said “A Bricolage index (‘1’–‘4’) may sit with the waymark.” PRINCIPLES banned “circled 01–04” and “equal columns” but then “countered” the tell with “a spine of pine waymarks with uneven step widths.” Codex took the index, ignored the uneven widths, and produced a textbook timeline.

**Fix, visual.** No numbers of any kind: not `1 2 3 4`, not `01–04`, not a circled index, not the word “Step.” Names only, in Hanken 500 or Bricolage 500 at Heading 3 size: Intro, Plan, Strategy, Handover. One continuous 2px pine spine (or a single quiet hairline behind the names) — not a waymark on every step. Strategy, the decisive step, sits in a **grey elevated** panel (`#E8E8E4`, radius 8 or 12, **no shadow**) and absorbs the fuller “Strategy your team can put to work” copy, so the separate card below is deleted.

**Fix, structure (short).** Do not use four equal columns. The other three steps are short and close to the spine; Strategy is larger. Crafted alternative: UX Lead, [`UX-CRITIQUE-V2.md`](/workspace/northway-v2/docs/UX-CRITIQUE-V2.md).

### 2. Case Ledger as numbered hairline rows (01–04, waymark, eyebrow, rule)

**The tell Amos named next.** Four identical rows: a two-digit Bricolage index, a waymark, a client, a one-line result, an uppercase `HEALTH · 2025` / `CONSULTING · 2025` / `STUDIO · 2025`, a hairline. Kindred also gets a giant “K” card on the right. It is a generated “projects list” component, not a studio’s selected work.

**We invited the indexes and the row waymarks.** We allowed uppercase sector/year without a count limit.

**Fix, visual.**

- **No index numbers. No waymark on rows.**
- **No uppercase eyebrow on every row.** Sector and year become a caption sentence in Hanken 400 muted: “Health, 2025.” The only tracked uppercase attribution on the page is the Briefing’s, once, plus “TYPICAL” on Grow.
- **Featured Kindred is editorial, not a row with a monogram.** An Excerpt panel (see tell 3) beside or above a larger setting of the client and the result. More height than its neighbours. Do not repeat the hero’s display sentence verbatim on this panel or this row — write a companion line or a named quote (“They made the next step obvious.” already exists later; it can live here once).
- **The other three are quieter text entries**, uneven measure, **space instead of hairlines** (a single rule under the last entry is allowed). Client in Bricolage 500 at Heading 3; result in Hanken body; caption in muted.

**Fix, structure (short).** Featured-versus-quiet, not four equal rows. “See all case studies” at the **end of the list**, not opposite the heading. UX Lead for the crafted layout.

### 3. Giant “K” monogram, twice

A huge Bricolage K, pine on a white rounded card, once peeking from the Briefing and once on the first ledger row. It is a logo-maker default pretending to be a photograph. It is the most obviously “generated asset” on the page.

**This is on us.** We specified the Folio: “a large Bricolage initial of the client, 120–160px, cropped by the card.” Withdrawn.

**Fix, visual — the Excerpt, which replaces the Folio.**

A grey elevated panel (`#E8E8E4`), radius 8 or 12, **no drop shadow, no monogram, no giant letter.** Inside: a named quote or a one-line result, set in Bricolage 500 at lead or Heading 2, ink, at a readable measure. Client and a caption underneath, Hanken 400 muted. An optional 3–4px pine tick on the left edge, **once**, short (not a full-height bar, and not repeated around the site). The panel is a pulled quotation from a briefing.

When a still exists, the photograph fills this same panel, clipped, caption below, **no letter over the image.** Kindred ships as a quote Excerpt, never as a “K.” Used in the peeking Briefing slot and for a featured no-image case. Quiet ledger rows do not need a panel; they are text-complete.

The peeking object at the foot of the Briefing is this Excerpt, overlapping the white page by 40–64px. It must not restates the display result word for word (see tell 9).

### 4. The waymark used as a bullet on everything

The 4px pine (or cream) bar appears on: the wordmark, every section heading, every ledger row, every process step, the Strategy card, the Pine Band close, and the footer wordmark. A device used this often is no longer a device. It is a stamp. Combined with the numerals, it is why the page feels “designed by a system.”

**We invited this.** BRAND-V2: “Used at section starts, ledger indexes, process steps, current nav, the wordmark,” and only then “Not a bullet on every heading.” Codex followed the first list.

**Fix, visual.** Reserve the waymark for **the wordmark, the current nav item, and at most one hero-level moment.** It is not a section-head prefix, not a list bullet, not a process marker, not a floating cream bar above the Pine Band heading. A heading is a heading. Process uses one spine, not four waymarks. If a featured Excerpt wants a short pine tick, that tick is a different, smaller atom, used once.

### 5. The same section-head pattern, five times

Almost every light chapter is: waymark + heading on the left + a text link or dek on the right (Case studies, Price, Process), or a close variant with the link under the heading (Insights, FAQ), often with a hairline. Once is a pattern. Five times is template anatomy.

**Fix, visual.** That exact left/right pattern may be used **at most once** on a page, if at all. Other sections: a heading, optional one-line dek **under** it, left-weighted, **no waymark.** “See all” is a text link at the **end of the content**, in Hanken 500, underlined, not a mirror of the title. Headings stay in Bricolage 500, sentence case, with a full stop if they are statements (“Know the price before we talk.”) — the writing is fine; the chrome around it is not.

**Fix, structure (short).** Vary the section openings. UX Lead is revising the “fixed section anatomy” rule and will craft the alternatives. Do not invent a second competing structure here.

### 6. Grey page, white floating cards — the palette inverted, and one card molecule everywhere

The canvas is `#F2F2EF`. Folio, Price Tablet and the Strategy card are identical white rounded rectangles with a soft shadow. That is a SaaS marketing page. Amos has inverted it: **the page is white `#FFFFFF`; grey is the elevated colour** (cards, the Price Tablet, inputs, the Enquiry Sheet, the Excerpt).

`#F2F2EF` on `#FFFFFF` is only **1.12:1**. It will not read as a raised surface. We adjust elevated to **`#E8E8E4`** (oklch 0.930 0.0053 106.5), the same cool family, **1.23:1** against white — visibly a grey panel, still calm. Hover `#E3E3DF`. Pine bands are unchanged.

**Fix, visual.**

- Canvas token → `#FFFFFF`. Nav after the Briefing is solid white, not grey. Light chapters do not alternate as grey slabs; the page is one white sheet.
- Elevated surfaces → `#E8E8E4`. The Price Tablet, the Excerpt, the Enquiry Sheet, and Strategy’s process panel are this grey.
- **No drop shadow by default.** The grey fill *is* the elevation. Shadows on white are how every card UI floats. Optional 1px decorative hairline (`#C8C8C3`, 1.68:1 on white — decorative, not a 3:1 chrome line). Fine-pointer hover may deepen the fill to `#E3E3DF` or the hairline; it does not “lift” a shadow.
- Do not apply this panel to every block. Quiet ledger rows, insights and FAQ sit directly on white. If everything is a grey rounded rectangle, we have only swapped the fill.

All required contrast pairs on the new surfaces pass. Tightest required text: muted on elevated **6.28:1**. Tightest required UI: input border on elevated **4.47:1**. Full table in BRAND-V2.

### 7. Outline proof chips in the Briefing

Two ghost-pill chips (“Six-week engagement,” “Two review rounds”) sit under the lede, next to a real white pill. They read as extra buttons, and as a SaaS “feature chip” row. We banned orbiting chips in MOTION and then specified “two quiet chips, 999-radius.” Codex built what we wrote.

**Fix, visual.** Withdraw the chips. One cream-muted sentence in Hanken 400 at 16–18, under the lede: “A six-week engagement, with two review rounds.” Not a chip, not a pill, not a row of tags. The from-price stays in the CTA cluster and is not repeated here. If there is no honest figure, omit the sentence.

Pills are reserved for the one primary (white on pine, pine on light). No outline pills, no pill tags, no pill nav items.

### 8. Uppercase eyebrows as a site-wide system

`KINDRED CLINIC · HEALTH · 2025` in the Briefing (acceptable, once). Then `HEALTH · 2025` on the Folio and on the first row, `CONSULTING · 2025`, `STUDIO · 2025`. Tracked uppercase meta on every case is a marketing-site eyebrow, even when the words are factual.

**We invited this** with a carve-out for “attribution, sector/year, and Typical,” and no cap on how often.

**Fix, visual.** At most **one** tracked uppercase attribution per page: the Briefing’s line. “TYPICAL” on Grow remains, once, because it is a non-colour cue, not an eyebrow. Everywhere else, sector and year are a caption sentence, sentence case, Hanken 400 muted, no dots, no tracking: “Health, 2025.” No uppercase line above section headings. CONVERSION’s carve-out should be tightened to this (listed under impacts in BRAND-V2).

### 9. The Kindred result, three times in one scroll

The display line, the peeking Folio, and the first ledger row all say “Kindred Clinic left with a plan their team could use in the first week.” A generated page repeats its best sentence because it has no second thought.

**Fix, visual.** The display line keeps the result (it is the lead). The peeking Excerpt uses a **named quote** or a shorter companion (“They made the next step obvious.” — already in the demo, currently under the Price Tablet). The featured ledger entry can then lead with client and a challenge or approach line, and point to the case. One sentence, one place, at display scale.

### 10. The Strategy card as a second, centred “quote card”

After the process grid, a large white rounded card is centred on a grey slab, waymark, “Strategy your team can put to work,” a paragraph, “About the studio.” It is the same molecule as the Folio and the Price sheet. It restates a process step. It is a leftover “highlight” block.

**Fix, visual.** Delete this card as a separate section. Fold its copy into Strategy on the Process Path, as the grey elevated panel in tell 1. “About the studio” can be a text link at the end of that panel, or the studio short can be a **left-weighted text block on white**, no card. If a named quote is needed, use one Excerpt, not a third rounded sheet.

**Fix, structure (short).** Do not insert a centred highlight between Process and FAQ. UX Lead for the studio-short alternative.

### 11. Hairlines as the only list rhythm

Ledger, Insights, Price rows and the FAQ accordion are all divided by the same light horizontal rule. Mechanical, even, indistinguishable.

**Fix, visual.** Prefer vertical space (24–40px) to a rule. A single hairline under the last item of a list, or under Grow only (in addition to the pine rule), is enough. Insights can be two or three short titles with a dek, uneven, no rules, no waymark. FAQ can keep a rule inside the accordion if it helps the hit target; it should not also inherit the ledger’s waymark-and-index language (it doesn’t, which is fine — just lose the matching weight of rule).

### 12. Decorative moments that all centre the same way

The Briefing and the Pine Band are **allowed** to be centred; that is the Eden calm we adopted, and it should stay. What tips the page into “everything is centred” is the combination of: a perfectly symmetric four-column process, a centred Strategy card on a grey slab, and a cream waymark bar floating above “Ready when you are.” in the same beat as the hero.

**Fix, visual.** Keep the two green bands centred. Process and studio short are left-weighted (tells 1 and 10). On the Pine Band, drop the floating cream waymark; a heading, a reply-time line and one white pill are enough. “Ready when you are.” is generic (we offered it). Visually the close works without it; if UX Lead prefers a more specific line (reply-time, or a result), we will set it the same way.

### 13. Insights as a copy of the Ledger molecule

“A few notes from recent work.” then three titles with waymarks and hairlines, “See all insights” under the heading. It is the Case Ledger at a smaller size.

**Fix, visual.** Titles in Bricolage 500 at Heading 3 or Hanken 500 at lead, a one-line dek in body, no waymark, no index, no hairline, uneven. “See all insights” at the end. No panel required.

**Fix, structure (short).** Not a third instance of the row component. UX Lead for the teaser layout.

### 14. The nav primary is too loud while the hero pill is in view

“Start a project” in the nav is underlined cream, visually a second CTA next to the white pill. The one-primary rule already exists. The underline is what makes it compete.

**Fix, visual.** While the Briefing’s white pill is on screen, the nav item is quiet cream Hanken 500, **no underline**, no pill, no heavier weight than “About.” After the band scrolls away, it becomes the pine pill on the white nav. This is a visual restatement of a conversion rule; UX Lead owns the behaviour.

### 15. Three “Start a project” labels on the Price Tablet

Grow has the pine pill, correctly. Position and Brand + growth use the same words as a text link. In one view, the phrase appears three times, plus the nav. It feels like a template that stamps a CTA on every row.

**Fix, visual.** Grow keeps the pine pill, one primary in this view (nav quiet if the pill is visible — same rule). The other two rows: the package name and the price are the target, or a quieter verb (“Enquire,” “Ask about Position”) if a link is required. Do not repeat the primary label.

**Fix, structure (short).** UX Lead is revising “a CTA in every section” and can settle the row targets.

### 16. Light sections have no material, only chrome

Grain on pine is present and right. The light half of the page has no equivalent sense of surface: no still, no Excerpt (only the monogram), no grey panel that feels like a document, just rules and letters. The answer is **not** grain on white, and **not** a decorative illustration style. The Excerpt, the grey Price Tablet, uneven type, and a real still when a buyer has one, are the material. Do not invent Kindred photography. Do not add a second texture language.

### 17. Smaller tells, still worth killing

- **Connecting timeline rule** on 1–4: part of tell 1; it is a kit component. It goes with the numbers.
- **FAQ “+” affordance:** acceptable (Phosphor, a job). Do not add a waymark. The “Before we start.” / “More questions” head is tell 5.
- **Nav first item is “Work”** in the screenshot; conversion names the page “Case studies.” Visual is fine either way; label is UX Lead’s.
- **Footer is sound.** Continuous pine, disclosure, slogan in its place, email, legal. Leave it. Quieter cream, no extra waymark beyond the wordmark if the wordmark already has one.
- **“TYPICAL”** is the right non-colour cue. Keep it. It is not an eyebrow in the banned sense, provided it appears once.

## Top five fixes for the v3 rebuild

If Codex can only do five things, these five remove the generated look. The rest of the list still applies.

1. **Remove every number.** No process 1–4, no ledger 01–04, no indexes of any kind. Names and writing only.
2. **Replace the Folio monogram with the Excerpt.** Grey panel, a quote or a result, no giant letter. Peeking object and featured Kindred both use it. Do not say the Kindred result three times.
3. **White canvas, grey elevated, no drop shadows.** `#FFFFFF` page, `#E8E8E4` for the Price Tablet, the Excerpt, the Enquiry Sheet and Strategy’s panel. Quiet rows sit on white. Stop the identical white floating cards.
4. **Discipline the waymark and the section heads.** Waymark: wordmark, current nav, at most one hero moment. The heading-left / link-right / waymark pattern at most once. Eyebrows: Briefing attribution once, plus “TYPICAL.” Captions everywhere else.
5. **Make the Process Path uneven and un-numbered**, and **delete the separate Strategy card** by folding its copy into Strategy’s grey panel. Proof in the hero becomes a sentence, not two outline chips.
